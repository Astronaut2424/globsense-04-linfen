03 — Sourcing

Three mountains. Three gestures.

Every ingredient is hand-harvested in a window of weeks, paid above market, and traced to a single grower.

Changbai · 长白
松脂 · Pine resin

Harvested at first frost from forty-year pines along the Korean border. The resin is hand-tapped at dawn, then aged six weeks in cedar. It carries the cold of the mountain into the bottle.

Wuyi · 武夷
油茶 · Camellia oil

Pressed cold from camellia seeds grown on the volcanic shelves of Fujian. A single grower, the Lin family, has farmed the same hectare for nine generations. Yield: eight litres per harvest.

Yunnan · 云南
金银花 · Honeysuckle

Picked in the last two weeks of April from terraced slopes outside Dali. Each flower is dried in shade, never sun. The top note is what your face notices first — bright, almost cool, then warm.
